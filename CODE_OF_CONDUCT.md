# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants should communicate constructively, assume good faith, and focus
on what is best for the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized conduct, threats, and publishing private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact
listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as
confidential as practical. Maintainers may remove content or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
